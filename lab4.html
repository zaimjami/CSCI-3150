Overview

Barfi! is a 2012 Indian Hindi-language romantic comedy film written and directed by Anurag Basu, produced by Ronnie Screwvala (UTV Motion Pictures), and starring Ranbir Kapoor, Priyanka Chopra, and Ileana D’Cruz. Set in 1970s Darjeeling and Kolkata, it follows Murphy “Barfi,” a deaf-mute young man, and his relationships with Shruti and the autistic Jhilmil. The film was acclaimed for performances, direction, cinematography, music, and its portrayal of disability; it became a major commercial success and is widely regarded as a modern cult classic.

India selected Barfi! as its official entry for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film. At the 58th Filmfare Awards, it received 12 nominations and won 7, including Best Film, Best Actor (Kapoor), Best Female Debut (D’Cruz), and Best Music Director (Pritam).

Plot (condensed)

1972: Barfi, raised by his chauffeur father in Darjeeling, falls for Shruti, who ultimately marries elsewhere under family pressure. To pay for his father’s surgery, Barfi bungles a bank robbery and then intercepts the kidnapping of his autistic childhood friend Jhilmil, sheltering her in Kolkata as a protective companion.

1978: Shruti reconnects with Barfi in Kolkata, creating tension with Jhilmil, who vanishes. After a ransom ruse, Jhilmil is presumed dead; Barfi is nearly framed, but inspector Dutta lets him go. Following clues, Barfi finds Jhilmil alive—the kidnappings were embezzlement schemes tied to her trust. Barfi and Jhilmil marry; Shruti remains wistful but supportive.

2012: In old age and ill health, Barfi and Jhilmil pass peacefully together.

Production

Anurag Basu developed Barfi! from a short story into a nonlinear, dual-period narrative, citing homages to Chaplin and Keaton via physical comedy and silent-cinema flavor. Principal photography ran 2011–2012 across Darjeeling, Kolkata, Mumbai, Coimbatore/Pollachi, and Ooty.

Kapoor’s performance drew on Chaplin-esque physicality; Chopra researched autism extensively for Jhilmil; D’Cruz debuted in Hindi cinema, also serving as the film’s narrator.

Reception & accolades

Critics praised the film’s performances (especially Kapoor and Chopra), direction, visual design, and score. The film earned strong domestic and overseas grosses (see “Box office”) and received numerous awards, including multiple Filmfare, Screen, and Zee Cine wins. It was India’s submission to the 85th Academy Awards.

Box office

Despite a limited opening, Barfi! posted high multiplex occupancy and sustained word-of-mouth, earning roughly ₹1.06 billion gross in India and about ₹1.75 billion worldwide, placing among the top-grossing Hindi films of 2012.
