Overview

Barfi! is a romantic comedy-drama set in 1970s Darjeeling and Kolkata. It follows Murphy “Barfi” (a deaf and mute young man), Shruti, and Jhilmil (on the autism spectrum), weaving a non-linear story about care, choice, and belonging. On release in 2012, the film earned critical acclaim for its performances, direction, cinematography, music, and humane portrayal of disability, and became one of the year’s commercial successes in India and overseas.

Plot

In Darjeeling (1972), prank-loving Barfi meets Shruti, who loves him but yields to family pressure to marry someone “safer.” Struggling to pay for his father’s medical care, Barfi crosses paths again with Jhilmil, an autistic childhood friend and heiress; the two form a gentle, protective bond and move to Kolkata. Years later, Shruti—unhappy in marriage—reenters Barfi’s life, triggering jealousy, a missing-person hunt for Jhilmil, and revelations about schemes surrounding her trust. Ultimately, Barfi and Jhilmil reunite and build a life together; in the present day, they pass away peacefully, side by side.

Production

Anurag Basu wrote the film during and after Kites, retaining a non-linear structure to keep a simple story engaging. Principal photography ran 2011–2012 across Mumbai, Darjeeling, Kolkata and the Nilgiris. Physical comedy and silent-era homages (Keaton/Chaplin) shape set-pieces while the narrative centers care and interdependence over ableist “fixing.”

Soundtrack

Pritam’s score folds in bossa-nova inflections and lilting themes; lyrics include work by Swanand Kirkire, Ashish Pandit, Neelesh Misra and Sayeed Quadri. The album released 9 Aug 2012 and was widely praised for its warmth and melodic through-lines.

Box office

Opening strong at multiplexes, Barfi! sustained multi-week legs and finished near ₹1.75 billion worldwide gross, ranking among 2012’s top Hindi releases domestically and overseas.
