About Me

I’m Zaim, a CS student at CU Boulder and a Software Engineering Intern at Spectrum.

I love turning messy ideas into clean, accessible interfaces that work with a keyboard, screen readers, and phones first. Colorado is home and I have been here since a little child at 2-years old—I recharge in the mountains, and ski whenever classes and code freezes allow. In the kitchen I bounce between Mediterranean staples and South Asian comfort food, which keeps my meal prep interesting. On campus I build nonprofit apps with Blueprint, where I’ve learned to ship for real users and iterate from feedback. Lately I’m deep into semantic HTML, responsive CSS grid/flex patterns, and measuring performance the boring-but-reliable way. I’m also leveling up my TypeScript/React patterns and testing, because good DX helps me move fast without breaking access. Away from the screen I read, take photos around Boulder, and try to be a better teammate and mentor each semester.
